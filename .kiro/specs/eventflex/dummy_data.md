Workshops and mini-sessions will also be conducted to enhance technical and presentation skills. At the end of the hackathon, teams will present their projects to a panel of judges based on innovation, feasibility, design, and impact.

Exciting prizes, networking opportunities, and recognition await the winners. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced coder, this hackathon offers a platform to learn, innovate, and showcase your talent.
